What's inside

More than just clicking a button

🎣

Cast & Catch

Hit the FISH button and see what bites. From worthless boots to legendary swordfish, every cast is a gamble.

🏪

Sell & Upgrade

Sell your haul at the shop, save up cash, and upgrade your fishing rod to unlock rarer catches.

🪱

Bait System

Buy normal bait to target specific fish, or try scammer bait for unpredictable results.

🤝

Fish Bargain

Enter the bargain minigame and bid against a rival buyer. Win the fish or lose your money trying.

⚔️

Clans & Quests

Create or join a clan, complete fishing quests together, and boost your catch rates as a team.

💾

Save Progress

Your game auto-saves. Back up your clan with restore codes so you never lose your crew.
